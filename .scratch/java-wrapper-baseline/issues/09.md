## What to build

Preview and record sales, including backdated entries, only when the affected holding remains nonnegative throughout its entire quantity history.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Create consistent upstream securities/cash entries, retain security definitions and transaction history after partial/full disposal, and use shared decimal amount/fees/taxes validation.
- [ ] Preserve explicit times; use 11:00:01 only for missing sale times and disclose the fallback. Never keep shifting times until a sale passes.
- [ ] At identical timestamps process quantity-increasing before quantity-decreasing transactions with a documented stable tie-breaker within each group. Validate the affected security/securities-account history at the proposed date and all later dates.
- [ ] Reject a sale that makes any affected historical balance negative with a clear notification and unchanged financial data; do not calculate or persist FIFO or profit.
- [ ] Verify partial/full sale, valid backdating, later-negative backdating, account isolation, explicit/fallback timestamps, ties, preview, persistence, and update candidate checks.
