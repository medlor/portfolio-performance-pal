## What to build

Inspect purchases, sales, and dividends across open and closed holdings while making ambiguous securities-account attribution visible.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Filter by security identifier/name, securities account, transaction type, and date range; sort by date with newest first by default, stable ties, limit 15 by default, and zero unlimited.
- [ ] Retain historical purchases/sales and security definitions after a holding closes; show recorded quantities/amounts without calculated profit or live valuation.
- [ ] Apply upstream reference-cash-account/security relevance rules for dividends, show the owning cash account, flag ambiguous securities-account attribution when accounts share the cash account and security, and display each dividend once.
- [ ] Verify account-isolated results, shared-cash-account ambiguity, closed holdings, filters, sort ties, limits, and immutable read behavior through the actual launcher/container.
